EU Anti-Money Laundering Regulation (AMLR)

On 10 July 2027, a single EU regulation replaces national rules on anti-money laundering and countering the financing of terrorism. Due diligence obligations become the same across the Union. The EU authority AMLA specifies them through technical standards and guidelines.

Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 of 31 May 2024 brings together in a single, directly applicable text the obligations of obliged entities: risk assessment, customer due diligence, identification of beneficial owners, suspicious transaction reports. It applies from 10 July 2027 and, for football clubs and agents, from 10 July 2029.

The regulation tasks AMLA with issuing several sets of guidelines by 10 July 2027, for example on outsourcing and on risks relating to third countries. AMLA is also consulting on draft technical standards that will apply from the same date.

Our view

A single regulation removes the differences between countries, but requires groups to review their procedures subsidiary by subsidiary. The heaviest part lies in the data: customer files to complete, beneficial owners, history of checks. Screening tools are only as good as the reference data feeding them.
