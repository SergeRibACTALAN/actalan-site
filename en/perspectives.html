IFRS 18

The work lies in the accounting chain, not just in the statements.

What is changing

IFRS 18 replaces IAS 1 for reporting periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027, with comparatives restated. The income statement is reorganised into categories (operating, investing, financing), with new subtotals and management-defined performance measures to be explained in the notes.

For supervisory reporting, revised FINREP will only apply from the September 2027 reference period; until then, the ECB lets institutions report under either IAS 1 or IFRS 18.

Our view

If the new presentation is produced through manual adjustments at closing, it will be costly every quarter and hard to audit. The categories should be carried by the accounting schemes and analysis axes, from the moment the entry is generated.

What we do

Impact assessment on accounting schemes and reports, adaptation of accounting engine rules, reconciliation of both presentations during the transition.

AMLR

Compliance will be won or lost on customer data quality.

What is changing

Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 applies directly from 10 July 2027. It replaces national transpositions of the directives with a single anti-money-laundering rulebook across the Union.

The technical standards that detail its implementation are still being adopted, under the new European authority AMLA.

Our view

Legal interpretation will soon be shared across the market. The difference will lie in the ability to prove: complete customer files, reliable reference data, alerts that can be explained.

What we do

Gap analysis, KYC remediation, customer reference data quality, tooling for controls and alerts.

IReF

A single statistical reporting framework in 2031: data preparation starts now.

What is changing

The Eurosystem aims to harmonise statistical reporting by euro area banks within a single framework. Under the timeline published by the ECB on 8 June 2026, a one-year pilot starts in the second quarter of 2030 and the first official reporting in the second quarter of 2031, with one year of parallel reporting.

This timeline still depends on the adoption of the IReF Regulation, following a public consultation planned for the second half of 2027.

Our view

More granular reporting requires knowing where each data point comes from, from the transaction to the submitted report. Institutions that have rebuilt this lineage, for BCBS 239 or their reconciliations, will start ahead.

What we do

Reverse engineering and data lineage, source inventory, reconciliations between accounting, risk and statistics.

Service centres

An accounting platform is best maintained by those who know it.

What we see

In many institutions, knowledge of the accounting engine rules rests with a handful of people. When they leave, or when maintenance is handed to a centre far removed from the business, every change becomes slower and riskier.

Our view

A useful service centre brings together experts who know your system, works in pairs so that it depends on no single person, and documents every rule it touches.

What we do

Maintenance and changes to accounting schemes, specialist support, documentation kept up to date with Actarule.
