T+1 settlement

On 11 October 2027, securities settlement moves from two days to one day after the trade in the European Union. Securities financing transactions are exempt. Regulation (EU) 2025/2075 amended the Central Securities Depositories Regulation to that effect.

Regulation (EU) 2025/2075 of 8 October 2025, published on 14 October 2025, amends the Central Securities Depositories Regulation. From 11 October 2027, transactions in securities traded on EU venues settle no later than the business day after the trade, compared with two days today.

Securities financing transactions, such as securities lending and repurchase agreements, are exempt, as are certain over-the-counter transactions. Two steps precede the switch (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1536): from 7 December 2026, allocations and confirmations transmitted in an electronic, machine-readable format; from 1 July 2027, place of trading in settlement instructions and reporting of the reasons for settlement fails.

Our view

With one day less, what used to be done the day after the trade has to be done the same evening: allocation, confirmation, settlement instructions, funding, foreign exchange. Overnight batches and next-day reconciliations come too late. Settlement fails are paid for in penalties.

What we do

Impact analysis on securities chains and accounting, adaptation of accounting schemes and reconciliations, monitoring of settlement fails and penalties.
