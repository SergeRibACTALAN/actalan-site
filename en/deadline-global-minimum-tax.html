The global minimum tax, stemming from the OECD rules and Directive (EU) 2022/2523, ensures effective taxation of at least 15% for groups whose consolidated revenue reaches €750 million. In France, the filing process comprises a notification, the information return, a tax computation statement and payment of the top-up tax.

The return, the statement and the payment are due within 15 months of year end: 31 March 2027 for a financial year ended 31 December 2025. For the first year of application, the deadline was 18 months, that is 30 June 2026.

Our view

The return relies on accounting and tax data by entity and by country, adjusted under rules that are neither those of the consolidated accounts nor those of corporate income tax. The first filing was often done on spreadsheets. The second is the time to industrialise data collection and keep the audit trail.

What we do

Collection and control of data by entity, reconciliation with the consolidated accounts and tax returns, documentation of adjustments.
