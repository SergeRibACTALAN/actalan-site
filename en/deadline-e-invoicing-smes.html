E-invoicing for SMEs and very small businesses

On 1 September 2027, SMEs and micro-enterprises in France must in turn issue their invoices in electronic format. Since 1 September 2026, all companies must already be able to receive them, and large and mid-sized companies must issue them.

The reform of electronic invoicing between businesses came into force on 1 September 2026. Since that date, all companies established in France and subject to VAT must be able to receive electronic invoices, and large and mid-sized companies must issue theirs in that format.

On 1 September 2027, the obligation to issue extends to small and medium-sized enterprises and micro-enterprises. The transmission of data to the tax administration follows the same timetable. Every company goes through a platform accredited by the State.

Our view

For a financial institution, there are two sides to this. On the supplier side, invoices already arrive in the new format. On the business side, smaller subsidiaries that invoice services come under the issuance obligation in 2027. One then needs to know which transactions are in scope and link them to accounting.
