The principle

From business event to journal entry.

An accounting interpreter applies your rules to every event produced by your business applications, and generates the entries.

Business event Your business applications Transactions, contracts, flows: the raw material of accounting.
Accounting rules A single repository Accounting schemes, by product and by standard.
Entries Detailed and balanced Each one stays linked to the original event.
Accounts and reports General and subsidiary ledgers Up to accounting and regulatory statements.
Several standards from a single feed.
Every entry can be traced back to the transaction.
Rules change without touching the business applications.
