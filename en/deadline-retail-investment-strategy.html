Retail Investment Strategy

The EU Retail Investment Strategy revises the rules on the design and distribution of savings products. The implementing measures are expected from July 2029. They cover the value for money of products, the remuneration of distributors and information for clients.

Parliament and the Council reached a provisional agreement on 18 December 2025. According to the timetable given by the Commission, the final votes were expected in the fourth quarter of 2026 and publication in the Official Journal by January 2027, followed by transposition within 24 months.

On 30 July 2026, the Commission asked ESMA for technical advice, due by 1 October 2027, on the level 2 measures: value for money, inducements, best interest of the client, suitability, key information document. These measures would apply from July 2029.

Our view

Value for money requires comparing, product by product, costs and performance against benchmarks. The data exist, but they are scattered between manufacturers, distributors and account keepers. Networks distributing third-party products will depend on the quality of the data they receive.

What we do

Inventory of cost and performance data by product, reconciliation between manufacturers and distributors, adaptation of reporting and information documents.
