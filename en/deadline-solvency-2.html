Solvency II review

On 30 January 2027, the Solvency II review applies. The new reporting templates and the directive on insurance recovery and resolution arrive on the same day. Capital calculation, governance and reporting change together.

What is changing.

Directive (EU) 2025/2 and Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/269 change the capital calculation: new risk margin, recalibrated market risk parameters, wider matching adjustment. A proportionality regime lightens the obligations of small and non-complex undertakings, and sustainability and liquidity risks enter the prudential framework.

On the reporting side, taxonomy 2.10.0 applies from the first quarter of 2027: several templates disappear (S.21, S.29, S.30), new natural catastrophe templates appear, and the annual submission deadline moves to 16 weeks for solo and 22 for group reporting. In France, the ACPR published instructions 2026-I-07 to 2026-I-14 in July 2026, setting out the new submissions.

Our view

Three workstreams fall on the same date: actuarial calculations, the QRT production chain and, for the largest undertakings, recovery plans. The risk is to handle each topic in its own silo and multiply manual adjustments between statutory accounts, the prudential balance sheet and the submitted templates.

What we do

Impact analysis on templates and data flows, adaptation of the reporting chain, reconciliations between accounting, prudential balance sheet and QRT, documentation of rules for auditors and the supervisor.
