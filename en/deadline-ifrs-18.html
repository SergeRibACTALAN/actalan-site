IFRS 18, a new presentation of profit or loss

IFRS 18 replaces IAS 1 for reporting periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027. The income statement is reorganised into categories, with new subtotals. The way profit is measured does not change; the way it is presented does.

Comparatives

The previous period is restated under the new presentation.

The standard, endorsed by the European Union on 13 February 2026 (Regulation 2026/338), reorganises the income statement into categories (operating, investing, financing), creates new subtotals and requires management-defined performance measures to be explained in the notes. Comparatives must be restated.

For prudential reporting, FINREP adapted to IFRS 18 only applies from the end-September 2027 reference date. Until then, the ECB lets institutions report under either IAS 1 or IFRS 18. In France, the Banque de France schedules the corresponding taxonomy releases on eSurfi.

Our view

If the new presentation is produced through manual adjustments at closing, it will be costly every quarter and hard to audit. The categories should be carried by the accounting schemes and analysis axes, from the moment the entry is generated.
